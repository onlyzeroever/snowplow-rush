Snowplow Rush privacy policy

Last updated: date of publication

Draft for Alex to review, Nov 21, 2026. Not linked from the game yet. Highlighted items need a decision or a detail only Alex has. This draft was written to match how the game works today and the plan for the apps; it is not legal advice, and a short check by someone who knows UK and EU privacy law is worth it before launch.

Snowplow Rush is a game about clearing snow. We want you to enjoy it without worrying about your data, so we collect as little as we can, we never ask for your name, email or contacts, and we never sell anything about you.

Who we are

Snowplow Rush is made by legal name of the developer or company, country. For anything about privacy, email contact address, for example privacy@snowplowrush.app. We answer within 30 days.

What we collect and why

WhatWhyWho handles it
Anonymous play data: a random ID made by the game on your device, which jobs you play, how they go (time, stars, how much snow you cleared), which screens you open, what you buy or watch in the game, your device type, screen size, language and time zone, and error messages. To see where the game is too hard, too easy or broken, and to improve it. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not store your location or IP address with it. PostHog (analytics), servers in the USA
Recordings of your first plays (web version): a low-frame-rate recording of the game screen during your first two visits, for up to 20 minutes each. Text fields are hidden. To watch where new players get stuck. PostHog apps: on or off, Alex to decide
Feedback you send from the game. To read it. Please leave out your name and contact details. PostHog
Your progress: the saved game. So you can carry on where you left off. Stored on your device. In the apps, also in your own iCloud (iPhone, iPad) or Android backup, so it comes back on a new phone.
Purchases (apps): what you bought and when, with a random customer ID. To give you what you paid for and restore it on a new device. Apple or Google take the payment; RevenueCat keeps the purchase record. We never see your card details.
Ads (apps): optional video ads you choose to watch for a reward. The ad companies may use your device's advertising ID (only if you allow it on iPhone), IP address and device information to show and measure ads, and, where you agree, to show ads that suit you. To keep the game free. AppLovin (MAX) and Google (AdMob), and the ad networks they work with
Reminders (apps, only if you say yes): a note when your helpers are done, your daily gift is ready or a weekend event starts. To bring you back when something is waiting for you. Scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

Your choices

  • Turn off play data: Settings, then Share play data: off. The game then sends nothing to our analytics.
  • Ads: on iPhone, the game asks before an ad company can use your advertising ID; you can change it in iOS Settings, Privacy, Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings. In the UK and EU you are asked before any personalised ads.
  • Reminders: say no when asked, or turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your data: Settings, then Reset progress deletes your save. To delete the play data linked to your random ID, email us from the game's Settings screen: add a "Delete my data" button that sends the random ID, or show the ID in Settings so players can quote it.

How long we keep it

Play data: up to 12 months. Recordings: up to 30 days. Feedback: until we have read and acted on it, and no longer than 12 months. Purchase records: as long as the law requires for tax and refunds.

Legal basis (UK and EU)

We use play data and recordings because we have a legitimate interest in making the game work well, with the choice to turn them off. Before launch in the UK and EU: either ask for consent before analytics and recordings start, or confirm this basis with a privacy adviser. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent. Purchases are handled to fulfil our contract with you.

Children

Snowplow Rush is a game for everyone, but it is not made for children under 13 and is not in any store's kids section. If you think a child under 13 has sent us something, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top, and for big changes we will tell you in the game.
