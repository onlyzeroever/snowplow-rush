# Snowplow Rush: Splice list

Splice's licence covers sounds used in video games, including on their own as sound effects. The one thing it rules out is passing the raw sounds on to others as sound effects or samples, and a game doesn't do that. Download everything while your Splice plan is active.

**How to do it:** for each line, search Splice and download the 1–2 you like best. Short, clean, cartoony and cozy beats realistic. Name each file after the sound it's for (e.g. `bump_a.wav`, `bump_b.wav`), zip them and attach the zip here. I trim, level and key-match everything, and build the chimes from the instrument notes.

Good packs to start in: **Cartoon Land** (Blastwave FX), **Builder Game**, **Farm Game** and **Advanced Game Sounds** (Epic Stock Media).
